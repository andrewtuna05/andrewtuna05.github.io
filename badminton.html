My Badminton Career

I've dedicated more than a decade of my life to professional badminton. During this time, I've had the chance to travel across the world, experience so many different cultures and make many new friends!
Amongst my accomplishments include:

  • Represented USA at 2025 FISU World University Games at Rhine-Ruhr, Germany
  • Qualified to represent USA at 2021 FISU World University Games at Chengdu, China
  • Represented USA at 2022 World Junior Championships in Santander, Spain
  • Former US Junior Rank 1 in Singles, Doubles, and Mixed
  • US Adult National Finalist
  • Pan American Junior Champion
  • US Junior National Champion
  • 5x US Junior National Team
